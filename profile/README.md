## Lucyd

A prediction markets exchange for financial assets. Trade on where prices of crypto and other markets will go, from minutes to days ahead. Orders are matched on a central limit order book, and markets settle automatically on chain from oracle prices.

- **Trade:** [lucyd.trade](https://lucyd.trade)
- **API:** [api.lucyd.trade](https://api.lucyd.trade) for markets, trades, positions and trader stats
- **MCP server:** [mcp-server](https://github.com/lucyd-trade/mcp-server) lets AI agents such as Claude and Cursor browse markets and trade
