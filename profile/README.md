## Lucyd

A prediction markets exchange for financial assets. Trade on where prices of crypto and other markets will go, from minutes to days ahead. Orders are matched on a central limit order book, and markets settle automatically on chain from oracle prices.

Lucyd is live on the Sepolia testnet.

- **Trade:** [testnet.lucyd.trade](https://testnet.lucyd.trade)
- **API:** [api.testnet.lucyd.trade](https://api.testnet.lucyd.trade) for markets, trades, positions and trader stats
- **Smart contracts:** [contracts](https://github.com/Lucyd-trade/contracts): open source, verified on [Sourcify](https://sourcify.dev); market tokens, Pyth price settlement and payouts
- **MCP server:** [mcp-server](https://github.com/Lucyd-trade/mcp-server) lets AI agents such as Claude and Cursor browse markets and trade
- **Status:** [status.testnet.lucyd.trade](https://status.testnet.lucyd.trade)
